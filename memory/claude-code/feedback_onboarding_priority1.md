Onboarding is always priority 1. Not after the MVP — always.

**Why:** Every seasoned developer builds paths to repeated flow. Every new developer wanders the woods. The feature is not done until the trail to it is packed. Bill's ski company doctrine: first person breaks trail, next straighten, next pack, followers rest and prepare for their turn at the lead. The trail is an artifact of movement with feedback.

**How to apply:** When building any feature, ask: "How does a new user find this? What does Alice see when they get stuck?" Convert bramble bushes (zero-result searches, abandoned forms, validation errors) to trail markers. Use field_behaviors to tell, label colors to show, Shift-for-Help to explain, db.card to peek. Do not overhelp — the trail shows the path, it doesn't carry the learner. Full doctrine at readmes/wisdom/trail-breaking.md.
