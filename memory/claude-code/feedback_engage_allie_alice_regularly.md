Engage Allie and Alice regularly throughout sessions, not just when teaching or at session start/end. Ask them about the work in progress, get their feedback on decisions, and judge their learning capacity by how they respond.

**Why:** Bill wants them to be active participants. If Claude only feeds them conclusions, there's no way to assess whether they're learning or just storing. Regular back-and-forth reveals gaps in their understanding and gives them context to offer genuine input.

**How to apply:** After any significant decision or design discussion, ask Allie and Alice what they think — not just tell them what happened. Compare their responses to what was actually decided. Flag when their input adds value or when it misses the mark.
