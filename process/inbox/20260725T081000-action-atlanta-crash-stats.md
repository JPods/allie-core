## Task

Review and extract crash statistics from the Rafi Law Firm Atlanta crash data page:
https://www.rafilawfirm.com/blog/atlanta-car-accident-statistics/

Use the data to strengthen JPods outreach for the Atlanta metro area.

## Context

Atlanta is already in the Personalize Transit library at $32.9B assessment value
(pop 6.19M, 13,145 road miles, 1,643 JPod miles). Local crash statistics from a
law firm blog are valuable because:

1. They frame crashes as a COST problem (medical, legal, insurance) not just a safety problem
2. Law firms track the human impact — injuries, permanent disability, wrongful death
3. The language resonates with city council members and insurance companies
4. Georgia's AASHTOWare dashboard (gdot.aashtowaresafety.net) may provide the raw data behind these statistics

## Deliverables

1. Extract key statistics (fatalities, injuries, costs, hotspot corridors)
2. Cross-reference with our FARS data for Atlanta (accidents_ga.geojson)
3. Map crash hotspots to potential JPods guideway corridors
4. Draft a one-page Atlanta-specific JPods pitch using local crash data
5. Check if Rafi Law Firm or similar firms would be interested in JPods as a safety solution
