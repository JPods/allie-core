summary: An aspect that works in isolation does not mean it works when looped 40 times. Save signals (denorm, recalc, keyword build) that are fine for one record become blocking when iterated in a tight loop inside a transaction. The BOM seed command hung because each Item.save() and BillOfMaterial.save() triggers cascading post-save work that compounds across 40+ records. Always test iteration behavior separately from single-record behavior. Seed commands and bulk operations are where this surfaces first. Applies to all models with post-save signals, not just BOM.
