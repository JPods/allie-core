See It Before You Build It

JPods3D turns SketchUp into a transit design studio. Place stations on satellite terrain, connect guideways, validate the physics, and watch pods move — all before breaking ground.

For Students

Learn transit engineering by building networks in your own neighborhood. Place stations at real locations, connect them with solar-powered guideways, and simulate passenger flow. The plugin teaches physics, geometry, and infrastructure design through hands-on 3D modeling.

For Planners

Evaluate JPods networks against real terrain, traffic data, and crash corridors. Generate bills of materials. Test capacity at stations. Export simulation data for economic analysis. See what your city looks like with solar-powered transit above the streets.

Built-In Intelligence

JPods3D includes an agent team that validates, routes, and balances your network automatically.

🛰

Noelle

Network validator and load balancer. Reviews every build for safety, connectivity, and clearance height. Flags defects before you animate.

What Is JPods?

JPods is a circulatory system for a city. Blood does not run once a week — it runs continuously. JPods moves small packets streaming resources to where they are needed on demand.

— The Physical Internet

Middle Mile Transit

JPods is the WiFi of the Physical Internet — station-to-station, neighborhood-to-neighborhood. Solar-powered guideways above the streets, 500 kg pods on demand. Last-mile modes (bikes, walking, scooters) bridge from stations to front doors.

Cargo & Waste

Not just passengers. Pre-sorted goods from warehouses to neighborhood stations by day. Waste streamed continuously out of the city for sorting. Fresh waste sorted on arrival supports far higher recycling rates than weekly collection of rotted, compressed, mixed bags.

Data-Driven Network Design

MeshMobility analyzes crash data, traffic counts, and census data to determine where JPods networks are needed most. Crash corridors become guideway corridors. Walk circles become station placement.

💥

Crash Data

FARS fatal crash records mapped by location. Red circles show where people are dying on roads. Guideways follow crash corridors.

🚗

Traffic Counts

AADT data shows vehicle volume. High-count corridors with high crash rates are the strongest candidates for JPods networks.

👥

Walk Circles

15-minute walk radius from each station. When circles overlap with full coverage, the network serves the whole city. More doors, not bigger doors.
