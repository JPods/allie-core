 How it works:                                                                 
  - At animation start: builds ezones from every merge/diverge EP in lines.json
  - Every tick: checks if two pods approach the same merge junction             
  - Ring pod keeps full speed (priority)                           
  - Entry pod speed scales linearly: full at zone boundary (3m out), zero at    
  junction                                                                  
  - Every stop-wait logged as a fault for capacity analysis                     
  - Session totals printed at stop                                
                                                                                
  The EZoneDef object is rich and extensible — carries shape, timing, priority,
  and experience counters. Ready for future parameters as you learn what each   
  junction needs.                                                 
                                                                                
  Restart SketchUp (new file jpod_ezone.rb needs boot.rb to load it), Build, and
   test on 3+circle. You should see [Noelle] N ezone(s) built at animation start
   and entry pods slowing at merge junctions.
