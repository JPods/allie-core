▶ Workflow Guide
JPods Network — 8-Step Student Workflow

1. Geolocate — File › Geo-location
2. Place Structures — drag from library
3. CP Calculate — detect connection points
4. Connect Guideways — click CP pairs, W for waypoints
5. Build — bezier beams, columns, solar, network.json
6. Populate — pods at ~70% capacity
7. Animate — random dispatch through traffic circles
8. Review — Crew Health Check
