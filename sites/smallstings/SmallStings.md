# Small Stings — Retrospection Before the Funeral

**SmallStings.com | SmallSting.com**

## The Problem

Accountability in America is batch-processed. A pothole goes unreported for months. A crosswalk stays unmarked for years. A store loses customers who can't find what they need. Nothing happens until someone is hurt, sues, or leaves a bad Yelp review weeks later. All the cost comes at the catastrophic end.

## The Solution

Small Stings is packet-switching applied to accountability. Continuous tiny signals that force continuous tiny improvements — before the funeral, before the lawsuit, before the customer leaves.

**Yelp is retrospection after you leave. Small Stings is retrospection while you're standing there.**

A citizen fines the city $1 for an unmarked crosswalk. A customer stings a store because they can't find the toothpaste. Each sting is a timestamped, geolocated fiscal event — a record that proves the organization knew about the problem. Tiny financially. Massive as signal.

---

## How It Works

### 1. File a Sting
Open the app. Take a photo. Describe the problem. The sting is filed with GPS coordinates, timestamp, and your MyCarryOn identity. Cost to the organization: $1.

### 2. Organization Responds (or Doesn't)
- **If they respond:** Problem solved in real time. "Aisle 7, Shelf 3, left side." The sting is resolved. No fine. The customer is served. The signal is recorded.
- **If they don't respond:** The $1 fine stands. The record accumulates. The next sting at the same location is $2. The pattern builds.

### 3. Patterns Emerge
47 stings at one intersection this month → fix it this month, not after the next funeral.
47 people couldn't find the toothpaste → move it, don't wait for the quarterly review.
The aggregated stings design the solution. Citizens aren't just complaining — they're providing the data.

### 4. Constructive Knowledge
Every sting is a dated record that the organization knew about the risk. If someone is killed at a crosswalk with 23 prior stings, those stings are exhibit A. The organization cannot claim ignorance. This is the legal mechanism that makes Small Stings more than a feedback tool — it is an accountability infrastructure.

## Two Modes

### CityRoadkills Mode — Roads and Public Safety

Citizens fine the city for unsafe infrastructure:
- Unmarked crosswalks
- Blind intersections
- Missing barriers
- Broken signals
- Dangerous school zones

**What happens with the data:**
- Crash corridors emerge from sting patterns
- Noelle uses sting density for JPods station placement
- JPods pays citizens for sting data (network design input)
- Sting records become litigation evidence if someone is harmed
- Cities adopt 5x5 Free Market to allow privately funded alternatives

**Connection to CityRoadkills.com:** Every sting strengthens the city letter. The crash map shows where people died. The sting map shows where people are about to die — and that the city knew.

### Commerce Mode — Stores and Organizations

Customers sting businesses for service failures:
- "I can't find the toothpaste"
- "The bathroom is out of soap"
- "I've been waiting 45 minutes"
- "The price tag doesn't match the register"
- "This aisle is blocked"

**What happens with the data:**
- Alice routes the sting to the right employee in real time
- The store responds before the customer leaves
- Patterns accumulate: what fails, where, how often
- Every sting is an Alice observation that improves the business
- WC3 Desktop Hosting enables stores to run their own response system

**Connection to WebClerk:** When a store runs WC3, the sting becomes a two-way signal. The customer's app talks to Alice on the store's server. The store's inventory system answers the query. The sting is resolved before it becomes a fine. The data stays on the store's server — sovereign, not in a SaaS cloud.

## The Principle

**Juries are retrospection after a death. Small Stings are retrospection before a death.**

Both are the Wisdom of the Many applied to accountability. Both create records that the organization cannot ignore. The difference is timing — stings create the signal while there is still time to act.

The current system waits for the catastrophe, then punishes. Small Stings signals the risk continuously, in real time, from the people who experience it. The aggregated wisdom of all of us, with each of us acting in our own self-interest, is wiser than the wisest of us at identifying what needs to be fixed.

**Judgment not compliance. Wisdom not obedience.**

Theme parks are 3,000 times safer than DOT roads because every injury faces a jury. Small Stings applies the same principle at a smaller scale — every defect faces a fiscal event. The cost is tiny ($1). The signal is massive (pattern of 47 stings). The record is permanent (constructive knowledge).

Organizations that respond to stings in real time will outperform those that don't — the same way theme parks outperform DOTs. The market sorts it. The Wisdom of the Many drives it. No government mandate required.
