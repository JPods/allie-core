The Market — Profit by Converting Middle Mile from Cars to JPods

JPods cost per passenger-mile: $0.03 — 50× less than buses ($1.45), 25× less than trains, 10× less than cars ($0.38).
51% of trips are 1-10 miles. North America forces 92% into cars. 80% of trips can shift.

If we cannot remove 10,000 cars a day from a road network, we do not build.

JPods is the Middle Mile — Making Streets Safe

~1 mile of JPods guideways per 14 miles of roads. JPods handles station-to-station (Middle Mile). Walking, bikes, scooters, Uber, and local-use vehicles handle door-to-station (Last Mile). JPods is the WiFi of the Physical Internet — Last Mile modes are Bluetooth.

By removing 10,000+ cars from roads, JPods makes streets safe to walk and bike. If JPods rates are too high, people use the safe streets JPods created — the network self-regulates through market discipline.

City ROI beyond farebox: Reduced road maintenance (fewer heavy vehicles), increased property tax from walkable streets (20× tax revenue per acre vs parking), reduced crash costs, reduced emergency response, healthier residents. None of these are counted in the projections above.

Conservative assumptions: No electrical generation revenue. No cargo revenue. No advertising revenue. No city fiscal benefits (reduced road costs, increased property tax). All are upside not included.
