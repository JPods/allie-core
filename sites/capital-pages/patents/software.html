WebClerk as Desktop Hosting Platform — The Library Services Opportunity

Desktop Hosting is the modernization of Yellow Pages. Every small business needs to be found, display their products, take orders, and manage their operations. Yellow Pages charged for listings. Yelp charges for prominence. Both are top-down — the platform owns the customer relationship.

WebClerk inverts this. The business owns their store, their data, their customer relationships. WC_HQ (WebClerk Headquarters) provides library services — normalized product data, distribution agreements, landed costs — flowing downstream to every WebClerk installation. This is DynamicCatalogs: the data supply chain.

36 million small businesses in America. A florist in Providence, a bike shop in Tulsa, a fireplace installer in Minneapolis — each installs WebClerk on a Mac Mini, gets a free Cloudflare account, and has enterprise-grade commerce with Alice as their AI bookkeeper. The library service feeds them product data the way a library feeds readers books — curated, normalized, always current.

Revenue model: $29-99/month per business for library services (product data, supplier connections, distribution agreements). At 1% penetration of 36M businesses = 360,000 subscribers × $50/month = $216M annual recurring revenue. At a 10× SaaS multiple = $2.16 billion.

This is not fantasy — it's the model Bill James described in his Desktop Hosting book (Wiley, 2002). Apple invited him on two national tours to present it. The technology has caught up to the vision. Cloudflare provides the edge. The Mac Mini provides the server. WebClerk provides the software. Alice provides the intelligence. The business owns everything.
Library Services Revenue Model
Tier 1 — Free: WebClerk open source install, Alice basic, local-only
Tier 2 — $29/mo: Library product data feed, basic supplier connections, cloud backup
Tier 3 — $99/mo: Full DynamicCatalogs (distribution agreements, landed costs, cross-company sync), Alice advanced coaching, email validation, phone normalization
Tier 4 — $299/mo: WC_HQ services (campaign ROI, manufacturer collaboration, multi-location sync)
