**Intent:** Start small, iterate relentlessly to change the economic lifeblood from oil to ingenuity™.

## Objective

By November 1, 2026, set up and operate a JPods network at the Mall of America, staging deployment in Minnesota cities in 3 phases:

1. **Rideable 10 meters** — We already have the truss structures, motors, electronics, and software. We need to build a new pod, set it up, and man it. **Cost: $120,000.**
2. **Certified 170 meters** — We must build, certify, and operate. **Cost: $6 million.**
3. **Wave of adoption** — Free tools for any city to design and price their own network: [10xMakers.com](https://10xMakers.com), [PersonalizeTransit.com](https://PersonalizeTransit.com), [MeshMobility.com](https://MeshMobility.com), [JPods3D.com](https://JPods3D.com), and [WebClerk.com](https://WebClerk.com). Each city that designs a network is a potential $3 million NCC/LMC license.
4. **Expand networks** in the Twin Cities where there is less than a 5-year payback period at a cost of ~$20 million/mile. [JPods' ROI engine](https://jpods.com/roi) and network-level calculator.

**Conservative payback: 7 years. Greenville SC model: 2.1 years.** We do not count electrical generation revenue or cargo — both are upside.

## Solution

Grade-separate to remove repeated start-stop and suspend the vehicle from a guideway to cut vehicle mass so **costs drop 92%** (10X benefit). Warehouse robots on the scale of airports and malls — $0.03 per passenger-mile vs. $3.40 for car ownership.

JPods' patent builds on the 54-year success of the Morgantown PRT (Personal Rapid Transit) opened by President Nixon's daughter in 1972. **Zero deaths. Zero serious injuries. 100+ million passenger-miles.** The US has had self-driving cars for 54 years. JPods' patent [6,810,817](https://library.jpods.com/patent) ([others](https://library.jpods.com/patents)):

- Networks of self-driving cars: *"A method of controlling a transportation System for moving people, freight, and any combination whereof using a distributed network of intelligent devices without requiring the aid of a human driver"*

**Independently audited:** The [2010 Morgantown PRT Benefit-Cost Report](https://library.jpods.com/LMC/WV/morgantown/WVUPRTBenefitCostReportv2.pdf) confirms the operating economics outperform buses on the same routes.

## Business Structure — Isolated Risks

| Company | Mission | Capital type | Risk profile |
|---------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **JPods LLC** | Invention, standards, certification | None — earns tech fees + $3M licenses | Innovation risk only |
| **JPods Capital LLC** | Aggregates transactions, interfaces to capital | Service fees | Financial service risk |
| **NCCs** (state-level) | Build networks, sell certified guideways to LMCs | Construction capital: $13M/mile for 18-36 months | Construction risk — isolated per state |
| **LMCs** (city-level, ~100K pop each) | Operate networks, sell trips and cargo | Asset capital: $20M/mile for 20-30 years (bonds) | Operating risk — isolated per city |

The same engine as housing: builder builds with construction loans → sells to owner → owner refinances with long-term debt. CCs are the builders. LMCs are the owners. JPods certifies the transfer.
