1. **WebServing is the commercial engine for WebClerk adoption.** The routing app (apps/webserving/) makes local inventory discoverable. Free tier feeds the network; subscriptions fund infrastructure. The network effect does the selling — every store that joins makes every search more useful. Domain: [WC3] [Cross-domain]

2. **empoweringeveryone.com is the pitch page, webserving.com is the tool.** Two audiences, two URLs. Business owners and city planners go to empoweringeveryone.com. Customers searching for products go to webserving.com. The Apple/Mac Mini play is documented on the vision page. Domain: [Cross-domain]

3. **Flowcharts must be audited against code regularly.** 4 of 45 flowcharts were materially stale after just 8 days. The Athena flowchart was missing the entire document sanitization pipeline. Payment GL had wrong model names. Alice Architecture had wrong pricing. Staleness is invisible until someone checks. Domain: [WC3]

4. **Simplify the customer-facing story ruthlessly.** Bill removed JPods delivery, network effect, and subscription tiers from the WebServing flowchart. What remained — Problem → Solution → Search → Sovereignty — is the complete story. The red Amazon cost annotations (resource drain, customer surrender) made the argument visceral. Detail belongs on the vision page, not the product page. Domain: [Cross-domain]

5. **Dependency Discipline prevents legal gaps.** Every outside library needs acknowledgement in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md at the same time it enters requirements.txt or package.json. We found 5 unacknowledged packages (nameparser LGPL-2.1 was the most important). Same commit, always. Domain: [WC3]
