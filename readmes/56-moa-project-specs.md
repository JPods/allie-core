## The Argument

1. **Safety:** Morgantown PRT — 50 years, zero deaths. ASTM F24 certified. 3,000x safer than roads.
2. **Efficiency:** PEC 1.26 vs cars 676. Solar-powered. 1 MW per mile.
3. **Economics:** $20M/mile vs $50M-$1B light rail. 7yr payback conservative. Revenue from fares + advertising + data.
4. **Precedent:** Same lot, same permit type as Cirque du Soleil 2024. MOU already signed.
5. **Constitutional:** State sovereignty over internal improvements. 5X5 Standard gives private networks ROW access.
