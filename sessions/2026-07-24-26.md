## Lessons for Allie

1. **The dedup tool is a product** — server-side detection, scored candidates, merge/delete with Shift-click, Edit button, company lookup, LinkedIn search, Note→ for preserving data, dedup journal for recovery. Every WC3 user gets this. Alice learns from every merge decision. [WC3]

2. **Distillation is how the team gets smarter without getting more expensive** — Tier 3 (general LLM) teaches Tier 2 (agent's own LLM) teaches Tier 1 (hard algorithm). Knowledge flows down, cost goes to zero. The agents coach each new Claude session with distilled patterns, not raw data dumps. [Cross-domain]

3. **The report is the last mile of data** — if the user can't print it, email it, or hand it to someone, the data has no value outside the system. Alice must know how to create output forms as a core capability. [WC3]

4. **Import everything, flag uncertainty, preserve originals** — parse what you can, store what you can't in config.original. Match by email first, phone second, name third. Score candidates. Alice organizes the sorting of garbage and value. Users stress badly over messy data — organize the pile and the stress disappears. [WC3]

5. **Pending records paint a picture of flows** — they are the narrative of the business, not temporary queue items. Users keep them for months/years. Never a record lock without a dangling pending. Move to library after seasonal cycle (12mo). [WC3]

6. **One Document per contact for ALL communications** — not per transaction, not per thread. One pile, filtered views. Voice, email, typed, system — all in one timeline. Alice manages archival per business type. [WC3]

7. **The payback calculator is the last mile of the spreadsheet** — same formulas, presentation layer anyone can use. MeshMobility designs the network, the calculator prices it. Each city that runs this is a potential $3M license. [JPods]

8. **JPods patent portfolio floor is $50M** — 9 families, Physical Internet® trademark. Data center patent (Family #4) may be the most valuable single asset as AI power demand scales. Combined with software ($50-100M), total JPods valuation $206-576M supports the $220M raise. [JPods]

9. **Bottom-up due diligence** — provide AI prompts so investors discover the value themselves. Don't tell, don't sell — give them the tools to convince themselves. Same principle as MeshMobility for cities. [JPods]

10. **An ant eats an elephant with small bites and lots of friends** — JPods stations are edge computing nodes. Thousands of Pis learning locally, sharing patterns globally. The guideway carries pods AND data. hivetechnologies.com. [Cross-domain]
